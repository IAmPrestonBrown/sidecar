# Example outreach brief: small bakeries

This fictional brief ships with Sidecar as an example. The bakeries and people in it
are made up.

## Who to contact

Owners or head bakers who plan the daily bake themselves. One contact per bakery per
wave. Never mark a guessed email address as verified.

## Email

Subject: question about planning the daily bake

Hi [First Name],

My name is Alex, and I'm a student studying how small food businesses plan their
day. I came across [Organization] and would like to hear how you decide what to bake.

[Optional sentence]

Would you have 15 minutes for a call sometime next week? I'm only here to learn.

Best,
Alex

## Follow-up

Hi [First Name],

Following up on my note from last week. If a short call works, I'm happy to fit
around your schedule.

Best,
Alex

## Final note

Hi [First Name],

Last note from me. If someone else at [Organization] plans the bake, I'd be glad to
talk with them instead.

Best,
Alex

## Banned phrases

- quick chat
- touch base
- game changer
- revolutionary
- app
- software
